About

We are introducing the fundamental principles of graphic design, including composition, color, typography, and related concepts, within a technical environment. We develop visual problem-solving skills as they relate to specific examples and projects. The history and development of the graphic design profession will also be discussed.

Essential objectives

  1. Demonstrate knowledge of the principles of graphic design.
  2. Employ technical skills to create design projects and communicate ideas.
  3. Research, identify, and apply essential aspects of graphic design.
  4. Apply traditional studio design skills to a computer environment.
  5. Use appropriate terminology to describe graphic design techniques and processes through illustration and computer skills.
  6. Illustrate creative thinking to solve a variety of design problems.
  7. Explore professional opportunities in the field of graphic design.
  8. Select and apply typography that supports and enhances individual design projects.
  9. Examine, discuss, and critique artwork, including references to the art-historical, social, and cultural context.
  10. Design and complete individual projects.
  11. Create a portfolio of graphic design projects.
  12. Display finished works in a professional manner.

Join our community

We welcome fellow design enthusiasts, students, and professionals to join our community. Share your ideas, learn from others, and stay updated on the latest trends in the design industry.

Calendar 2026: Discover the Art of Organization

Dive into the creative world of Kart Production and Kasign with our latest 2026 Calendar Collection. This year, we are excited to showcase a beautifully curated set of calendars that blend stunning photography from Kartography with elegant, professional design. Whether you are looking for a deep monthly dive or a quick yearly overview, our collection is designed to keep you inspired and organized throughout 2026.

What’s new for 2026?

Our 2026 calendars are a collaborative masterpiece of photography and layout. All designs are converted into high-quality, print-ready PDF formats, making them perfect for home, office, or digital use.

Highlights of the 2026 collection

  • Stunning photography: Each edition features unique, high-resolution photos from Kartography — ranging from peaceful water reflections to bold cultural icons.
  • Versatile layouts: Choose between our 12-month deluxe calendars for detailed planning or our one-page yearly overviews for a quick glance.
  • Inspirational quotes & art: Start every month with a dose of motivation and a piece of art that brightens your space.
  • U.S. federal holidays: All major holidays clearly marked to help you plan your year effectively.

How to access your free calendar

  • Download: Use the button below to open our Google Drive folder. You can view all editions and download your favorites for free.
  • Personalize your year: Want to feature your own photographs in a custom design? We love collaborating! Reach out at khadanacharya@gmail.com or kindnessartthatmatter@gmail.com.

Thank you for visiting Kart Production | Kasign. We hope these 2026 designs bring a touch of creativity, peace, and organization to your daily life!

Quick printing tips

  • Quality first: Use “High Quality” or “Best” settings on your printer.
  • Paper choice: A heavier cardstock or matte photo paper looks best.
  • Actual size: Select “Actual Size” or “100% Scale” in your print settings so the margins are perfect.

Elevate Your Screen: Custom Wallpapers by Kartography & Kasign

Your smartphone is your most personal device — it should reflect your unique style. Kart Production is proud to introduce a new series of premium phone wallpapers designed to bring art and inspiration to your fingertips.

A creative synergy

  • The vision by Kartography: Every wallpaper features exclusive photography captured by Kartography. These aren’t stock photos; they are original, high-quality images that serve as the soul of each design.
  • The design by Kasign: Our design arm layers these images with meaningful quotes. Using a precise layout process in Microsoft Word, we ensure the text and imagery complement each other perfectly.
  • Format: All final designs are delivered in high-definition PNG and JPEG formats, optimized for modern smartphone displays.

Explore the gallery

View the Wallpaper Collection on Google Drive

How to download & personalize

To make sure you receive the highest resolution for your device, we handle distribution personally.

  1. Browse the collection in the link above.
  2. Email us at khadanacharya@gmail.com with the name or description of the design you want.
  3. We will send the high-quality file directly to your inbox.

Need a custom design? If you want a specific quote paired with a Kartography image, or you have a unique vision for your brand, Kart Production is here to help. Email khadanacharya@gmail.com to start your custom project.
